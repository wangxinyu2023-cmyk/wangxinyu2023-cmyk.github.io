7 projects  /  architecture  /  installation  /  urbanism

Studio buildings, an interactive installation, a speculative city and an urban plan for Porto.

01

Osborn Plaza Market Canopy

A fleet of deployable bamboo-and-reed carts that turns a Brownsville plaza into a Wednesday farmers’ market, then folds down into everyday shaded seating.

Fall 2026  /  Senior studio / MidtermOpen project
02

The Vertical Village

Affordable housing stacked above a public library. A glass-and-timber core joins the reading floors, and walkable glass plates in the corridors pull daylight down through the homes.

Spring 2026  /  Studio / Library + housingOpen project
03

Between Levels

A common street across three levels: a hillside neighbourhood building where a shared stair-street, galleries and 48 public rooms connect streets 12 m apart.

2026  /  Self-initiatedOpen project
04

Threshold Apparatus

An interactive moving-image study of bodily proximity: a sensor reads how close you are to a doorway, and a projected mesh wakes, deforms and settles as you approach, pause and pass.

2026  /  Interactive installationOpen project
05

Human Race Motor

A city grown by rules instead of drawn by hand: cellular automata generate unit towers, the best are selected as the shell, and a space city takes shape around four zones and walkways in zero gravity.

2026  /  Speculative urbanismOpen project
06

Triangle Commons

A multi-purpose building for the New York Botanical Garden: triangular pavilions on slender columns, rotated off one triangular grid, stepping down a slope to the water and bridging the road beside it.

Spring 2025  /  Studio / Multi-purpose buildingOpen project
07

From Urbanity to Nature

A wine culture community on the last unbuilt stretch of Porto’s Douro riverfront: vineyards, orchards, a winery, homes and a hotel woven into the park, so the journey to port wine ends inside the city.

Urban designOpen project
