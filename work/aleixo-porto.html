From Urbanity to Nature

A wine culture community on the last unbuilt stretch of Porto’s Douro riverfront: vineyards, orchards, a winery, homes and a hotel woven into the park, so the journey to port wine ends inside the city.

Scroll
Type
Urban design / Wine culture community
Location
Aleixo, Porto, Portugal
Tools
Mapping, diagrams, collage

Porto has been transformed over the last decade, and the Douro riverfront has drawn the most building. Aleixo, a social housing complex under demolition, is the last front of the river without construction: a green valley with a natural cliff dropping to the river and the sea. Should we build there, and how can nature and architecture be balanced?

Visitors come to Porto for its wine, yet the vineyards and wineries are far from the centre. The project keeps the green texture of the land but turns it into urban agriculture, and adds a winery, homes, a trade centre and a hotel, so that wine tourists and locals meet in one community: production and consumption, nature and architecture, making and enjoying.

01A journey through the site

02City to site

Porto is one of the most popular cities in Europe and famous for its wine, but the vineyards and wineries visitors want to see are far from the centre.

Three things make Aleixo the site: a green park with abandoned buildings and a natural cliff over the river and the sea; the last unbuilt front on the Douro; and the tourist tram that passes through it. The Douro, the river of wine, is drawn in red.

1 more in the viewer

03Strategy

The site was a green area with two walls. The structure preserves the landscape and opens to the city from every corner. Organic and strong paths merge into one journey that ends at the pier or at the green amphitheatre of the park.

Visitors can walk over the buildings, through the landscape, or from one to the other, as the ground gradually sinks; the path offers the landscape to every sense.

04Plans and sections

05Five senses

The journey is designed around five senses. Smell: the aromas of grapes, fruit and vegetables through the year. Touch: the ups and downs of the landscape and the textured walls on the way to the winery. Sight: from the top of the winery, a panoramic view of the Douro and the pier.

Taste: wine and local cuisine with the best views. Hearing: wind through the trees, and water running from the highest point along the winery down to the pond in the park.
