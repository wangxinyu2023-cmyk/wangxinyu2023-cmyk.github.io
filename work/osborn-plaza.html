Osborn Plaza Market Canopy

Year
Fall 2026
Type
Senior studio · Midterm
Location
Brownsville, Brooklyn, NY
Tools
Rhino 8, Python, Ladybug, Photoshop

Osborn Plaza sits on a raised block of the former Osborn Street. It has trees, murals and movable cafe tables, but little shade in the late afternoon, exactly when a neighbourhood market would open.

The project proposes one deployable unit that works in three modes. On market day fifteen units line a single aisle from the south gate to Belmont Avenue; the rest of the week ten of them drop to bench height and give the plaza shaded seats. Bamboo carries, reed shades, planting cools.

01One cart, two lives

One cart, two lives: the bamboo and reed canopy arrives folded, deploys on site and lifts into a market table. Fifteen units fill Osborn Plaza for the Wednesday farmers' market; ten stay on as shaded benches the rest of the week, traced here through a July day of real sun and shadow.

    02The market street

    Stalls face each other across a 3.5 m aisle, after the Main Street market model. Vegetables and fruit anchor the gate; an info, EBT and health desk and a kitchen demo sit in the south-east court; the planted middle stays open for classes.

    Every unit was placed by script against the surveyed planters, trunks, benches and lanterns, and a 1.8 m accessible route was checked from the gate to every stall front.

    04Sun and shade

    Peak summer, June to August, noon to 5 pm, simulated with Ladybug Tools and Radiance on the site model and a LiDAR model of the neighbouring buildings. The market canopies cut solar radiation on the plaza by 21% and on the ground beneath them by two thirds; the everyday benches cut it by 13%.

    On a 92 F July afternoon the whole plaza is in strong heat stress, with or without canopies, but very strong heat stress (UTCI above 38 C) drops from 21% to 9% of the plaza. The reed lets 30% of the sun through, trading a little shade for airflow.

    05Material and structure

    A hybrid frame keeps five aluminium spine rails that carry the lift mechanism and replaces the wings with laminated bamboo, cutting frame emissions by about half while storing carbon.

    Planted boxes at both ends bring back the green, cooling edge of the first study. Every joint is bolted, pinned or clipped, so worn parts are swapped rather than repaired; bamboo rails sit on the mast heads through stainless knife plates and isolated bolts.

    06Urban systems

    Food, health, heat, mobility, housing and water around the plaza, read at four scales: the city, Community District 16, the five-minute walk and the plaza itself. Every map is drawn from NYC open data at a true scale.

    Grocery stores are within 300 ft, yet premature death is twice the city rate; heat vulnerability is 5 of 5; seven in ten households have no car; 83% of homes within a five-minute walk are NYCHA. The canopy is a walking market and daily shade for its neighbours, not more retail.
