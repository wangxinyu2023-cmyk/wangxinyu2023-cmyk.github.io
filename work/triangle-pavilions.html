Triangle Commons

A multi-purpose building for the New York Botanical Garden: triangular pavilions on slender columns, rotated off one triangular grid, stepping down a slope to the water and bridging the road beside it.

Scroll
Year
Spring 2025
Type
Sophomore studio / Multi-purpose building
Location
New York Botanical Garden, Bronx, NY
Tools
Rhino, rendering, Photoshop, physical model

On a slope in the New York Botanical Garden in the Bronx, between a road and the water, the site is first filled as a solid, then cut by a triangular grid. Triangles are aligned to the grid, duplicated and rotated, and the leftover gaps become a continuous route: up from the road, over a bridge, through the decks and down to the water.

Enclosed triangles hold a library, workspaces and a cafe deck with planted roofs. Between them, perforated dark canopies on a forest of thin white columns shade an open plaza, where the light changes from morning to afternoon.

Design process
